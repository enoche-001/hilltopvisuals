Questions

Hilltop Visuals offers a full suite of creative services including Graphic Design (logos, flyers, banners, posters), Web Development (landing pages to multi-page websites), Brand Identity packages, Social Media Graphics, and Print Design. If you need something creative, chances are I can help — just reach out!
It depends on the scope. A logo or flyer design can be ready in 1–3 days. A full brand identity package typically takes 3–7 days. Website projects range from 3 days for a simple landing page to 2–4 weeks for a full multi-page site with a custom backend. I always discuss timelines upfront so there are no surprises.
Simply reach out via the contact form below, WhatsApp, or any of my social platforms. Share a brief of what you need — the service, your brand, timeline, and budget if you have one. I'll respond within an hour to discuss next steps. No long waits, no fuss.
Absolutely. While I'm based in Lokoja, Kogi State, I work with clients across Nigeria and internationally. All communication, file delivery, and project management can be handled fully online via WhatsApp, email, or any preferred platform.
You'll receive all the formats you need. For logos and graphics: PNG (transparent background), JPG, PDF, and SVG. For print materials: high-resolution PDF or TIFF. For web assets: optimised PNG, JPG, and WebP. Just let me know what you need and I'll prepare everything accordingly.
