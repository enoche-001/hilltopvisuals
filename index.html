Hilltop Visuals is a creative design and web development studio in Lokoja, Kogi State, Nigeria. We exist to help businesses, startups, and entrepreneurs build memorable brands that drive real results.

We bring together sharp strategy, bold aesthetics, and technical precision to deliver design work that doesn't just look great — it works. From a single logo to a complete digital presence, we treat every project as an opportunity to create something extraordinary.

Questions

Hilltop Visuals offers a full suite of creative services including Graphic Design (logos, flyers, banners, posters), Web Development (landing pages to multi-page websites), Brand Identity packages, Social Media Graphics, and Print Design. If you need something creative, chances are we can help — just reach out!
It depends on the scope. A logo or flyer design can be ready in 1–3 days. A full brand identity package typically takes 3–7 days. Website projects range from 3 days for a simple landing page to 2–4 weeks for a full multi-page site with a custom backend. We always discuss timelines upfront so there are no surprises.
Simply reach out via the contact form below, WhatsApp, or any of our social platforms. Share a brief of what you need — the service, your brand, timeline, and budget if you have one. We'll respond within an hour to discuss next steps. No long waits, no fuss.
Absolutely. While we're based in Lokoja, Kogi State, we work with clients across Nigeria and internationally. All communication, file delivery, and project management can be handled fully online via WhatsApp, email, or any preferred platform.
You'll receive all the formats you need. For logos and graphics: PNG (transparent background), JPG, PDF, and SVG. For print materials: high-resolution PDF or TIFF. For web assets: optimised PNG, JPG, and WebP. Just let us know what you need and we'll prepare everything accordingly.
